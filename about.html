Hey, I'm Melody 💋

I'm a Cleveland girl, born and raised, and I'm loud about it. This is my little corner of the internet: game-day polls, horror talk, looks you vote on, and a whole lot of opinions about my teams.

Cleveland sports, all of them

Browns Sundays, Cavs nights, Guardians summers, and Ohio State football on Saturdays. I love every Cleveland team, even when they make it hard. Every week before kickoff I ask my fans how the Browns game will end, then I post what you called vs. what actually happened.

Scream is my forever favorite, and Saw is right behind it. I'll watch the classics, the slashers and the remakes, and I'll argue about the rankings with anyone. Some of my favorite polls have been horror fights, like the best Scream opening and my Halloween movie pick.
