The game

Both teams came in 5-2 with the AFC North lead on the line. Pittsburgh won it 27-6 at Acrisure Stadium, so the Steelers are 6-2 and we're 5-3.

Andre Szmyt kicked two field goals for our only points. The Steelers sacked Deshaun Watson eight times. Denzel Ward picked off Aaron Rodgers in the third quarter, and our last drive got stopped at the 1-yard line as time ran out.
